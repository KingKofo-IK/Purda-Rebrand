About Us

Welcome to Purda House of Fashion

Our story starts in Nigeria's fabric markets and sewing workshops, where hands-on apprenticeships with local artisans built a lasting respect for craft and detail. That grounding shaped everything we make today — from wedding-guest AsoEbi to prom gowns and full bridal looks.

Every piece carries that heritage forward, stitched with the same care and intention passed down through generations of skilled hands. We design inclusively, for every body and every story, because we believe personal style deserves to be celebrated rather than standardized.

Our Services

How To Order

01

Initial Inquiry

Send us your style inspiration, event date, dress size, and location so we can share a price estimate before booking your consultation. Reach us by Instagram DM or email at purdadesigns@gmail.com.

02

Custom-Made, Start to Finish

Every dress is built around your actual measurements, with production scheduled around your event date and the complexity of your design. Need it sooner? Rush production may be available for an additional 20–35% fee — just let us know your deadline when you reach out.

03

Consultation & Fitting

We finalize your sketch together, guide you through accurate measurements, and — for out-of-town clients — run the consultation over video call. You'll just need a tape measure and a second pair of hands. In-person fittings are also available at our studio.

04

Nationwide Shipping

Dress prices don't include shipping. Delivery within the U.S. is a flat $25.

Please note: because many of our gowns have limited or no stretch, it's important to hold your weight steady during the sewing period — significant changes either way can affect the final fit.
