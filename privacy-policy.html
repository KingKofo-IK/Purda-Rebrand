Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

This policy explains what information Purda House of Fashion ("Purda," "we," "us") collects when you contact us, submit a consultation inquiry, or communicate with us by phone, text (SMS), or email, and how we use and protect it.

Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide: name, email address, phone number, and mailing/shipping address
  • Event and order details: event date, category (Prom, Bridal, Custom/Occasion), size, budget range, delivery preference, and how you found us
  • Inspiration photos you upload as part of a consultation inquiry
  • The content of messages you send us by email, text, or through our website forms

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry, schedule consultations, and provide quotes
  • To send order/appointment confirmations and updates by email and text
  • To answer common questions automatically via our text and email auto-reply system
  • To process payments and fulfill custom orders
  • To improve our services and communicate with you about your project

Text Messaging (SMS)

We do not share or sell your mobile phone number to third parties for marketing purposes. Your number is used solely to communicate with you about your Purda House of Fashion inquiry, consultations, and order updates.

Message frequency varies depending on your inquiry and order status — typically a handful of messages around booking, consultation, and order updates. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. You can resume messages at any time by replying START. For help, reply HELP or contact us at purdadesigns@gmail.com. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Third-Party Service Providers

We use trusted third-party services to operate our business, which may process your information on our behalf strictly to provide these services:

  • Twilio — sends and receives text messages
  • Resend — sends confirmation and notification emails
  • Cloudflare — hosts our backend automation
  • Calendly — manages consultation booking and scheduling

These providers do not use your information for their own marketing purposes and are contractually limited to using it to provide services to us.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to fulfill your order, respond to your inquiries, and comply with our legal and accounting obligations.

Your Choices

You may ask us to update, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by emailing purdadesigns@gmail.com. You can unsubscribe from text messages by replying STOP, and from emails using the unsubscribe link where provided.
